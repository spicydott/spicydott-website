Comforting Chole

Slow-cooked chickpeas in a bold, aromatic gravy. A simple home-style chole that pairs beautifully with roti, bhatura or rice.

⏱ 45 min 👨‍👩‍👧 4 servings ✨ Easy

🌶️ The Spicy Dott touch

For the signature flavour, use Spicy Dott Chole Masala. Adjust salt and chilli to your taste and let the masala cook properly with the base before adding the main ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1½ tbsp Spicy Dott Chole Masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp dried kasuri methi
  • Fresh coriander
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Method

  1. If using dried chickpeas, soak overnight and pressure-cook until tender. Drain and reserve some cooking liquid.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté onions until golden. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add tomato puree, turmeric and chilli powder. Cook until the mixture thickens and oil begins to separate.
  4. Stir in Spicy Dott Chole Masala and cook for another minute to release its aroma.
  5. Add chickpeas and water. Simmer uncovered for 15–20 minutes, lightly crushing a few chickpeas to thicken the gravy.
  6. Finish with kasuri methi, coriander and lemon juice. Serve hot with bhatura, roti, rice or naan.

Serve it your way

Serve hot, garnish generously with fresh coriander and a squeeze of lemon. Enjoy the aroma of freshly cooked spices and make it a family favourite.
