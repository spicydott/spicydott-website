Authentic Pav Bhaji

A buttery, spicy and comforting Mumbai-style pav bhaji made at home with the rich flavour of Spicy Dott Pav Bhaji Masala.

⏱ 40 min 👨‍👩‍👧 4 servings ✨ Easy

🌶️ The Spicy Dott touch

For the signature flavour, use Spicy Dott Pav Bhaji Masala. Adjust salt and chilli to your taste and let the masala cook properly with the base before adding the main ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled
  • 1 cup cauliflower, finely chopped
  • 1 cup green peas
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small capsicum, chopped
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1½ tbsp Spicy Dott Pav Bhaji Masala
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Fresh coriander
  • 8 pav buns

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes, cauliflower and peas until soft. Mash them together and keep aside.
  2. Heat butter in a wide pan. Sauté onion until soft, then add capsicum and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes, chilli powder and Spicy Dott Pav Bhaji Masala. Cook until the tomatoes become soft and glossy.
  4. Add the mashed vegetables and a little water. Mash and mix well while simmering for 10–12 minutes.
  5. Adjust salt and consistency. Finish with lemon juice and fresh coriander.
  6. Toast the pav with butter and serve hot with bhaji, chopped onion, coriander and lemon.

Serve it your way

Serve hot, garnish generously with fresh coriander and a squeeze of lemon. Enjoy the aroma of freshly cooked spices and make it a family favourite.
