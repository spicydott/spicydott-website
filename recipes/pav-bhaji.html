Authentic Pav Bhaji

A buttery, spicy and comforting Mumbai-style Pav Bhaji made at home with the rich flavour of SPICY DOTT Pav Bhaji Masala.

⏱ 40 min 👨‍👩‍👧 4 servings ✨ Easy

🌶️ The SPICY DOTT Touch

For the signature Pav Bhaji flavour, use SPICY DOTT Pav Bhaji Masala. Cook the masala properly with the vegetables to release its aroma and authentic taste.

Ingredients

  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled
  • 1 cup cauliflower, finely chopped
  • 1 cup green peas
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 small capsicum, chopped
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1½ tbsp SPICY DOTT Pav Bhaji Masala
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Fresh coriander
  • 8 pav buns

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes, cauliflower and green peas until soft. Mash them together and keep aside.
  2. Heat butter in a wide pan. Add the chopped onion and sauté until soft. Add capsicum and cook for another 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes, Kashmiri chilli powder and SPICY DOTT Pav Bhaji Masala. Cook until the tomatoes become soft.
  4. Add the mashed vegetables and a little water. Mix and mash everything together.
  5. Simmer for 10–12 minutes. Add salt and adjust the consistency according to your preference.
  6. Finish with lemon juice and fresh coriander.
  7. Toast the pav with butter and serve hot with the bhaji, chopped onion, coriander and lemon.

🍋 Serve & Enjoy

Serve the Pav Bhaji piping hot with buttery toasted pav, finely chopped onion, fresh coriander and a squeeze of lemon.

Perfect for family evenings, weekend snacks, children's parties and special occasions.
