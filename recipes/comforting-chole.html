Comforting Chole

Slow-cooked chickpeas in a bold, aromatic gravy. A simple home-style chole that pairs beautifully with roti, bhatura, naan or rice.

⏱ 45 min 👨‍👩‍👧 4 servings ✨ Easy

🌶️ The SPICY DOTT Touch

For an authentic and aromatic chole, use SPICY DOTT Chole Masala. Allow the masala to cook with the onion and tomato base so its flavour and aroma develop beautifully.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 2 medium onions, finely chopped
  • 2 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1½ tbsp SPICY DOTT Chole Masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp chilli powder
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp dried kasuri methi
  • Fresh coriander
  • 1 tsp lemon juice

Method

  1. If using dried chickpeas, soak them overnight and pressure-cook until tender. Drain and keep some of the cooking liquid aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Add the chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add tomato puree, turmeric and chilli powder. Cook until the mixture thickens and the oil begins to separate.
  5. Add SPICY DOTT Chole Masala and cook for another minute to release the aroma of the spices.
  6. Add the cooked chickpeas and water. Mix well and simmer for 15–20 minutes.
  7. Lightly crush a few chickpeas while cooking. This helps make the gravy thicker and richer.
  8. Finish with kasuri methi, fresh coriander and lemon juice.

🥘 Serve & Enjoy

Serve the chole hot with bhatura, puri, roti, naan, paratha or steamed rice.

Garnish with fresh coriander, chopped onion and a squeeze of lemon for an extra burst of flavour.

Perfect for breakfast, lunch, dinner or a relaxed weekend family meal.
