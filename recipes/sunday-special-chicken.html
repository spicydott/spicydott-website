Sunday Special Chicken

A rich, aromatic chicken curry made for relaxed Sunday lunches, using SPICY DOTT Sunday Special Masala for a deep homemade flavour.

⏱ 55 min 👨‍👩‍👧 4 servings ✨ Medium

🌶️ The SPICY DOTT Touch

For a rich homemade chicken curry, use SPICY DOTT Sunday Special Masala. Allow the masala to cook well with the onion and tomato base before adding the chicken.

Ingredients

  • 750 g chicken pieces
  • 2 large onions, finely sliced
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp SPICY DOTT Sunday Special Masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 1½ cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, turmeric, a little salt and 1 tbsp SPICY DOTT Sunday Special Masala for about 20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a heavy pan. Add the sliced onions and cook slowly until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add tomatoes, chilli powder and the remaining SPICY DOTT Sunday Special Masala. Cook until the tomatoes soften and the masala begins to release oil.
  5. Add the marinated chicken and cook on medium heat for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally so every piece is coated with the masala.
  6. Add water, cover and simmer until the chicken is completely tender and the gravy reaches your preferred consistency.
  7. Finish with lemon juice and fresh coriander. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

🍗 Serve & Enjoy

Serve this aromatic chicken curry hot with steamed rice, jeera rice, roti, naan or paratha.

Perfect for Sunday family lunches, celebrations and special homemade meals.
