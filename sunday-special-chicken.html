Sunday Special Chicken

A rich, aromatic chicken curry made for relaxed Sunday lunches, using Spicy Dott Sunday Special Masala for a deep homemade flavour.

⏱ 55 min 👨‍👩‍👧 4 servings ✨ Medium

🌶️ The Spicy Dott touch

For the signature flavour, use Spicy Dott Sunday Special Masala. Adjust salt and chilli to your taste and let the masala cook properly with the base before adding the main ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 750 g chicken pieces
  • 2 large onions, finely sliced
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp Spicy Dott Sunday Special Masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 1½ cups water
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, turmeric, a little salt and 1 tbsp Sunday Special Masala for 20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a heavy pan. Brown the onions slowly until golden, then add ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Add tomatoes, chilli powder and the remaining Sunday Special Masala. Cook until the masala releases oil.
  4. Add marinated chicken and cook on medium heat for 8–10 minutes, stirring so the pieces are well coated.
  5. Add water, cover and simmer until the chicken is tender and the gravy reaches your preferred consistency.
  6. Finish with lemon juice and coriander. Rest for 5 minutes and serve with rice, roti or naan.

Serve it your way

Serve hot, garnish generously with fresh coriander and a squeeze of lemon. Enjoy the aroma of freshly cooked spices and make it a family favourite.
